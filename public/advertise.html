Advertise on HedwigPost 📢

Reach engaged, purchase-ready readers across tech, news, entertainment, and more.

HedwigPost is a growing independent publication covering Tech, News, Entertainment, How-To Guides, and Product Reviews. Our readers are curious, engaged, and actively looking for products, tools, and services that improve their lives — making our audience an ideal target for advertisers and brand partners across a wide range of industries.

Why Advertise With Us?

We are building a community of loyal readers who return to HedwigPost for trustworthy information and honest recommendations. Unlike broad social media audiences, our readers arrive with intent — they are searching for specific answers, researching purchases, or looking for tools to solve real problems. Advertising on an intent-driven content website consistently delivers better engagement than passive social media placements.

Our content spans multiple high-value categories: technology and gadget reviews attract buyers who are close to a purchase decision, our how-to guides attract readers who need specific tools and services to complete a task, and our product review section builds direct purchase intent in every reader who lands on it.

Advertising Options

  • Display Advertising: Banner and display advertisements served through Google AdSense on most pages. For direct display advertising placements, please get in touch.
  • Sponsored Content: We accept a limited number of sponsored articles, reviews, and features from brands that align with our editorial values. All sponsored content is clearly labelled. Our editorial team writes all sponsored content — we do not publish unedited copy.
  • Product Reviews: If you would like your product considered for an editorial review, you may send us a review sample. Receipt of a sample does not guarantee a review or a positive review — we publish honest assessments only.
  • Affiliate Partnerships: If you operate an affiliate programme, we may be interested in featuring your products within relevant articles on a commission basis.
  • Newsletter Mentions: Our newsletter is distributed to opted-in subscribers who receive curated content recommendations. Newsletter sponsorships offer direct, distraction-free access to our most loyal readers.

Our Editorial Standards

Our editorial independence is non-negotiable. HedwigPost does not publish content that we would not stand behind as genuinely useful and honest for our readers. Any advertising or sponsorship arrangement is entirely separate from our editorial decision-making. We do not adjust or distort our editorial opinions in exchange for advertising revenue. This standard is what our readers trust us to uphold, and it is what makes advertising on our platform valuable in the first place.
