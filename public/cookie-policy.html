Cookie Policy 🍪

Last updated: March 21, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how HedwigPost at hedwigpost.page ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our Website. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, to make them more efficient, and to provide information to website owners. Cookies are stored either for the duration of your browser session (session cookies) or for a longer period (persistent cookies, which remain on your device until they expire or you delete them).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

a) Strictly Necessary Cookies — These cookies are essential for the basic functioning of our Website. They enable core features such as page navigation and access to secure areas. The Website cannot function properly without these cookies.

b) Performance and Analytics Cookies — We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Website. Google Analytics sets cookies to collect information about visitors, pages visited, time spent, and referral sources. This data is aggregated and anonymised. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

c) Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense) — Our Website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense places cookies on your device to serve ads relevant to you based on your browsing history. The DoubleClick cookie is one of the primary cookies used by Google for advertising personalisation. You can manage or opt out at adssettings.google.com or www.aboutads.info.

d) Functional Cookies — These cookies allow our Website to remember choices you make (such as preferred theme) and provide enhanced, personalised features.

e) Social Media Cookies — If you use social sharing buttons on our Website, those platforms may set their own cookies governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies.

3. Cookies Set by Third Parties

In addition to our own cookies, several third parties may set cookies on your device when you visit our Website. These include Google (Analytics and AdSense), social media platforms, and embedded content providers (such as YouTube). We do not control these third-party cookies.

4. How Long Do Cookies Last?

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies last for a defined period — Google Analytics cookies last between 24 hours and 2 years. Advertising cookies typically last between 30 days and 2 years. You can view and delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. How to Control and Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences through:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to control cookies through Settings or Preferences. You can refuse all cookies, get notified when a cookie is set, or delete cookies when you close your browser.
  • Opting Out of Google Advertising Cookies: Visit adssettings.google.com or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Industry Opt-Out Tools: Visit www.aboutads.info (US) or www.youronlinechoices.eu (EU/UK).

6. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our Website, you will be shown a cookie consent banner. By continuing to use our Website after seeing this notice, or by clicking "Accept," you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You may withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your cookies and adjusting your browser settings.

7. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
